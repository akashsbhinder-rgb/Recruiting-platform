This page shows the standard terms of the Recruiting Agreement every Employer signs before hiring through MakePlacement. It’s published here for transparency — the actual signed copy is executed separately with each Employer, with the blanks below filled in.

This Recruiting Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of the date signed (the “Effective Date”) by and between:

MakePlacement, with a principal place of business at [MakePlacement business address], email akashmplacement@gmail.com (“MakePlacement”),

and

[Employer legal name], with a principal place of business at [Employer address] (“Employer”).

MakePlacement and Employer may be referred to individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

1. Agreement

Employer engages MakePlacement to identify and present candidates for employment (“Candidates”). Employer will provide MakePlacement with information about the role, including title, responsibilities, and proposed compensation. MakePlacement facilitates the presentment of Candidates through independent Recruiters using the MakePlacement platform; MakePlacement is not itself responsible for sourcing or pre-screening Candidates beyond what the platform’s Terms of Use describe.

2. Fees

Employer agrees to pay MakePlacement a fee of 25% of the Candidate’s first-year base salary for each Candidate successfully placed into a role with Employer through the platform. “First-year base salary” means the employee’s base salary, excluding discretionary bonus or equity.

Fees are due within fourteen (14) days after the Candidate signs an employment offer. Invoices unpaid after that period accrue a late fee of 3% per month. If an invoice remains overdue for more than two (2) months, MakePlacement may pause Employer’s access to the Services until the balance is paid in full.

3. Free Replacement Policy

If a placed Candidate leaves or is terminated within the first 90 days of employment, Employer will receive the full placement fee back as platform credit, to be applied toward a future placement fee. If a placed Candidate leaves or is terminated after 90 days, MakePlacement has no further obligation or liability with respect to that placement.

4. Candidate Ownership

If a Candidate is presented to Employer and Employer has already independently interviewed that Candidate within the 12 months before the presentation, Employer must notify MakePlacement of this within 10 business days of the presentation. If Employer does not notify MakePlacement within that window, the Candidate will be treated as sourced by MakePlacement if a hire later occurs.

If Employer declines or does not act on a Candidate presented through the platform, but hires that Candidate within 12 months of the presentation, Employer must notify MakePlacement promptly after signing the hire, and the fee described in Section 2 applies.

5. Confidentiality

MakePlacement acknowledges that Employer may share non-public information about its business, employees, hiring plans, and compensation (“Confidential Information”) in the course of this Agreement. MakePlacement will not disclose Confidential Information to unauthorized third parties or use it for any purpose outside providing the Services. This obligation does not apply to information that is public, already known to MakePlacement, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

6. Relationship of the Parties

MakePlacement is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or legal representative of Employer, except for the limited purpose of presenting and pre-screening Candidates. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship between the Parties.

7. Indemnity

Each Party agrees to indemnify and hold the other harmless from damages, liability, and reasonable legal costs arising from that Party’s own fault in connection with the services under this Agreement, except where a court finds the other Party’s own bad faith, gross negligence, or willful misconduct caused the damage.

8. No Poaching

Employer agrees not to solicit or hire any current employee of MakePlacement, or any Recruiter first introduced to Employer by MakePlacement, during this Agreement or for twelve (12) months after it ends. If Employer breaches this section by hiring such a person, Employer agrees to pay MakePlacement 30% of that person’s first-year salary.

9. Off-Platform Engagement

Employer agrees that any Recruiter introduced, matched, or otherwise connected to Employer through the MakePlacement platform will only be engaged for recruiting services through the platform. Employer will not directly or indirectly solicit, engage, or contract with such a Recruiter outside the platform for twelve (12) months after Employer’s most recent active use of the platform.

10. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement in writing (a) if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 14 days of written notice, (b) if the other Party is unable to perform its obligations, including payment, or (c) with 14 days’ written notice for any reason. Sections 2–5 and 8–9 survive termination.

11. Non-Solicitation

MakePlacement will not solicit any Candidate who has been employed by Employer, during this Agreement and for one year after.

12. No Discrimination

MakePlacement will not discriminate against any Candidate on the basis of race, national origin, religion, age, sex, sexual orientation, marital status, ancestry, pregnancy or parental status, disability, or veteran status.

13. General Provisions

Governing law and venue. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California and applicable federal law. Both Parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of state and federal courts located in California.

Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other’s written consent.

Amendments; waiver. This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both Parties. Failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of that or any other provision.

Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the rest of the Agreement remains in effect.

Entire agreement. This Agreement, together with MakePlacement’s Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Services.

Force majeure. MakePlacement is not liable for failure to perform due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, civil or military authority, riots, or natural disasters.

Notices. Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by mail or email to the addresses above.

Execution

This Agreement is executed by authorized representatives of both Parties at the time an Employer completes onboarding with MakePlacement.
