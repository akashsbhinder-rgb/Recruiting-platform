Recruiting Agreement

Terms of use Privacy Policy Recruiting Agreement

A contract (a “Recruiting Agreement”) is formed between makeplacement and a Referrer when the Referrer (1) creates an account on makeplacement, and (2) accepts the Terms of Use. No separate signature is required. Only once a Recruiting Agreement is formed can a Referrer submit a Referral.

1. Referrals

A “Referral” is the submission of a Candidate by a Referrer that meets all of the following:

  1. The Referrer has spoken to the Candidate about the specific Role and confirmed the Candidate is interested in that opportunity with the Employer;
  2. The Referrer has provided accurate contact information for the Candidate;
  3. The Referrer has provided a complete, current resume for the Candidate;
  4. The Referrer has explained why the Candidate is a good fit for the Role; and
  5. The Candidate is not a Duplicate Referral (below).

Hiring Cycle and payout eligibility

If a Referral is hired by the Employer while the Role remains open on makeplacement (the “Hiring Cycle”), makeplacement will pay the Referrer the bonus listed on the Role, payable after makeplacement receives payment from the Employer. If a Referral is still in process with an Employer after the Role is closed, the Referrer keeps ownership of that Referral until it is hired or rejected, and is still paid if it results in a hire. If a Candidate was already in the Employer’s interview process before the Referrer’s submission date, ownership belongs to the Employer, not the Referrer.

Duplicate Referrals

A Referrer has the exclusive right to the bonus resulting from a hire they referred during the Hiring Cycle for that Role. Any submission of the same Candidate by another Referrer during the same Hiring Cycle for the same Role is a “Duplicate Referral” and will be rejected. Priority is determined by the submission timestamps recorded in makeplacement’s database. Appeals can be sent to akash@makeplacement.com.

Sharing Role information

A Referrer may publicly share an Employer’s name or a Role’s salary range — including on professional networking sites, social media, personal websites, or elsewhere — to source Candidates. However, a Referrer may not repost a makeplacement Role on another job board (e.g. LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor) disguised as coming directly from the Employer, and may not misrepresent themselves as an employee of the Employer or of makeplacement. Referrers should identify themselves as a “makeplacement Referrer.”

Violating any part of this section is grounds for termination of the Recruiting Agreement, including account suspension.

2. Non-Solicitation

While a Referrer maintains an active makeplacement account, they will not maintain or pursue direct recruiting engagements with Employers discovered through makeplacement — including submitting candidates, sourcing roles, or entering recruiting agreements with those Employers outside the platform. For twelve (12) months after account termination, the Referrer will not initiate contact with or solicit those Employers for recruiting purposes.

While actively recruiting for a given Employer through makeplacement, and for six (6) months after that engagement ends, a Referrer will not directly or indirectly solicit or recruit that Employer’s employees to other companies. This applies across every Employer the Referrer is actively working with. Breach of this section may result in immediate account termination and forfeiture of any unpaid referral bonuses.

makeplacement may notify an Employer of a Referrer’s obligations under this section and request that the Employer not engage that Referrer outside the platform during the restricted periods.

3. Payment Terms

  • Once a Candidate signs the Employer’s offer, the Employer must pay makeplacement all outstanding fees within fourteen (14) days.
  • Once makeplacement receives that payment, the Referral bonus is credited to the Referrer’s makeplacement account.
  • To protect both Referrer and Employer, payouts follow an installment schedule tied to the 90-day guarantee period described in Section 4: one third released 30 days after the Candidate’s start date, one third at 60 days, and the final third at 90 days.
  • Referrers may withdraw available funds at any time via Stripe, our payment processor, using a payout method of their choice.
  • By using the Services, a Referrer authorizes makeplacement to share their submitted account information with Stripe and relevant financial institutions solely to process payment.

makeplacement is a third-party settlement organization and may report payments made to a Referrer to the IRS as required by law. Beyond that reporting, makeplacement does not withhold taxes on a Referrer’s behalf; each Referrer is solely responsible for their own tax filings and payments. makeplacement may request tax documentation from a Referrer to comply with its own reporting obligations.

Payment questions can be sent to akash@makeplacement.com.

4. Replacement Search Policy

This section applies if a placed Candidate leaves within 90 days of their start date, for any reason, including resignation, termination, or failure to start — except where the Candidate’s departure is due to the Employer’s own layoff, workforce reduction, or elimination of the role. In that case, the Referrer keeps any amount already paid and has no further obligation under this section.

Within two (2) days of makeplacement’s notice, if the Employer agrees to a replacement search, the Referrer must choose one of the following:

Option A — Conduct a replacement search

  • Confirm acceptance and provide a sourcing plan within two (2) days;
  • Submit at least three (3) qualified Candidates within fourteen (14) days;
  • Keep the pipeline active — no more than five (5) consecutive days without a new submission after day 14;
  • Complete a new placement within sixty (60) days.

A successful placement within 60 days requires no repayment. If unsuccessful or incomplete by day 60, makeplacement may grant an extension at its sole discretion upon the Referrer’s written request.

Option B — Decline the replacement search

The Referrer must immediately repay 100% of any placement fees already paid out for that placement.

Repayment

Repayment of 100% of fees already paid is required if the Referrer or the Employer declines a replacement search, the replacement search is unsuccessful or incomplete after 60 days without an approved extension, or the Referrer commits a material breach (including abandonment, bad faith conduct, circumvention, or non-responsiveness).

makeplacement will first deduct any amount owed from pending or future payouts to the Referrer. If no payout is available or it isn’t enough, the Referrer must remit the balance within seven (7) days, bearing any transaction fees. If a Referrer fails to repay, makeplacement may refer the balance to collections, report it to credit bureaus, and pursue legal remedies; the Referrer is responsible for related collection costs and attorneys’ fees.

5. Scope of Relationship

A Referrer is an independent contractor of makeplacement. This Recruiting Agreement does not create an employment, partnership, or agency relationship. A Referrer has no authority to enter contracts on behalf of makeplacement or any Employer. makeplacement does not supervise, direct, or control a Referrer’s work, set their hours or location, or provide training or equipment. makeplacement does not withhold taxes on a Referrer’s behalf, as it would for an employee.

A Referrer is free to provide recruiting services to others, including makeplacement’s competitors, subject to Section 2 above.

6. Miscellaneous

A Referrer will keep records sufficient to document compliance with this Recruiting Agreement, including payment and tax obligations, and provide copies to makeplacement on request. makeplacement may audit a Referrer’s records for compliance, no more than annually.
