This Privacy Policy explains how makeplacement (“makeplacement,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and Services, as described in our Terms of Use. If you have questions about this Policy, contact us at akash@makeplacement.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

CategoryExamplesSource
Account information Name, work email, password (stored in hashed form, not in plain text) You, at signup
Employer information Company name, location, hiring goals, employee count, open roles and role details Employer users
Referrer information Agency/firm name, contact details, submissions and activity on the platform Referrer users
Candidate Information Name, contact information, resume file, work history, and application status Submitted by a Referrer on the Candidate’s behalf — see Section 4

2. How we use information

  • To operate the marketplace — matching Employers, Referrers, and Candidates, and facilitating submissions, messaging, and hiring workflows;
  • To create and maintain accounts, and to authenticate users;
  • To calculate and collect placement fees owed under the Terms of Use;
  • To communicate with you about your account, submissions, or the Services;
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Services; and
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers on the Services.

3. How we share information

Sharing information between users is core to how the Services work. For example:

  • A Candidate’s information is shared with the Employer(s) a Referrer submits them to;
  • An Employer’s role and company details are shared with Referrers, subject to any approval step the Employer has configured;
  • Messages sent through the Services are shared with their intended recipient(s).

We also share information with service providers who process it on our behalf to operate the Services — currently, Supabase, Inc., which provides our database, authentication, and file storage infrastructure. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights, property, or safety of makeplacement or others, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Candidate Information

Candidates do not create accounts on makeplacement. Their information is submitted by a Referrer, who is required under our Terms of Use to have obtained the Candidate’s consent before submitting it. If you are a Candidate and want to know what information has been submitted about you, request a correction, or request deletion, contact us at akash@makeplacement.com and we will work with the submitting Referrer to address your request.

5. Data security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures, including those provided by our infrastructure provider, to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you close your account or request deletion, we will delete or de-identify your information unless we’re required or permitted to retain it — for example, records needed to substantiate a placement fee already owed.

7. Cookies and tracking

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in and to save in-progress form data so you don’t lose it if you navigate away. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not track your activity across third-party sites, so we do not respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals. Fonts and code libraries used by the Services may be loaded from third-party content delivery networks (such as Google Fonts), which may receive standard technical request information (like your IP address) as a normal part of serving that content.

8. Your rights and choices

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your account information at any time by contacting us at akash@makeplacement.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law — for example, California residents may have rights to know, delete, or correct personal information, and to opt out of certain uses, to the extent those laws apply to us. You can exercise any such rights by contacting us at the email above, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

9. Children’s privacy

The Services are intended for business use by adults and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, contact us at akash@makeplacement.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Your continued use of the Services after a change takes effect means you accept the updated Policy.
