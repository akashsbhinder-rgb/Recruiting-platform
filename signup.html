Frequently asked questions

What is MakePlacement?
MakePlacement connects companies hiring for a role with independent recruiters who work candidates in that space. Companies post what they need, recruiters submit candidates who match, and you only pay when you hire someone.
Is there a cost for recruiters to use MakePlacement?
No. It's free for recruiters to use. Companies pay a placement fee only when they hire a candidate who was submitted through MakePlacement.
What happens if a hire doesn't work out?
Every hire is covered by a 90-day replacement guarantee. If it doesn't work out within that window, MakePlacement runs a replacement search at no additional cost. Full terms are in the Recruitment Agreement.
How is this different from a staffing agency?
Instead of working with one agency, you get submissions from many independent recruiters at once, each competing to bring you the best fit. You review candidates and only pay for the one you actually hire.
Do recruiters have to work exclusively with MakePlacement?
No — recruiters can work with other clients too. Once you're introduced to a company through MakePlacement, though, you keep working with that company through the platform rather than moving the relationship off it.
Can I use MakePlacement alongside my own hiring process?
Yes. Posting a role on MakePlacement doesn't require you to route all of your hiring through us — it just adds another source of vetted candidates.
